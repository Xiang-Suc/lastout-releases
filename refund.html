Refund Policy.

Last updated: 2026-10-27 · English is the binding version; 中文为译本，两者不一致时以英文版为准。

If Lastout isn't right for you, you can get a full refund within 14 days of your purchase, no questions asked. The 14-day free trial lets you try it first, but the refund applies whether or not you used the trial.

How to ask for one

Write to support@lastout.dev with your order number, or reply to your receipt email from Paddle.com Market Ltd (Paddle). Orders are processed by Paddle.com Market Ltd (Paddle), our merchant of record, which issues the refund to your original payment method. On card statements the charge reads PADDLE.NET* ROCHA TECH (PAYPAL *PADDLE.NET with PayPal). Refunds usually reach you within a few business days, depending on your bank.

If you bought directly from us, write to support@lastout.dev or reply where you bought, and we refund you ourselves, to the payment method you used.

After a refund

The refunded license key is revoked: it can no longer activate Lastout on any Mac, and your license, for the App and the command-line tools, ends. Within about 15 minutes the App stops copying on every Mac where the key was activated, and so do the command-line tools' paid commands; the trial on those Macs ends too. You may then delete the App. Your recordings stay on your Mac.

Your legal rights

This policy doesn't limit any right to a refund or withdrawal that the law where you live gives you.

退款之后

被退款的授权码会被吊销：不能再在任何 Mac 上激活 Lastout，你对 App 和命令行工具的授权也随之终止。大约 15 分钟内，每一台激活过该授权码的 Mac 上的 App 停止复制，命令行工具的付费命令也一样停下；这些 Mac 的试用也随之结束。之后你可以删除 App。你的记录仍然保存在你的 Mac 上。
