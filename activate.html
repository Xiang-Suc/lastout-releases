If nothing opens

  1. No Lastout on this Mac yet? Download for macOS, unzip, move it to Applications and open it once. Then click Open Lastout above again.
  2. Lastout older than 0.4.2 opens but leaves the field empty. Copy the key, open Lastout again from Applications or Spotlight to bring up its Settings, go to Pass → Activate Lastout…, paste the key and click Activate.
  3. The same key works on up to 2 Macs; Deactivate on this Mac frees a seat.

The key is only in this page's address: nothing here sends it anywhere, and Lastout checks it with the license server only when you click Activate. Questions: support@lastout.dev.

没反应的话

  1. 这台 Mac 还没装 Lastout？下载 macOS 版，解压后拖进「应用程序」文件夹，打开一次，再点上面的「打开 Lastout」。
  2. 0.4.2 之前的 Lastout 会打开，但不会填入授权码。复制授权码，从「应用程序」或聚焦搜索再打开一次 Lastout，会弹出设置窗口；进入「通行证」→「激活 Lastout…」，粘贴授权码，点「激活」。
  3. 同一个授权码最多可以用在 2 台 Mac 上；点在这台 Mac 上停用可以腾出一个名额。

授权码只在这个页面的地址里：这里不会把它发到任何地方，Lastout 也只在你点「激活」时才向授权服务器核对。有问题请写信到 support@lastout.dev。
